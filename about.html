About Digitl Cats

Digitl Cats is not a real shop. It is a sandbox website owned by the Digitl Digital Analytics team, used to test tracking implementations and Claude skills without touching any client website or client data.

The site has a complete GA4 ecommerce dataLayer, custom events in the Digitl standard structure, a cookie banner with Google Consent Mode v2, and switchable test scenarios with deliberate tracking mistakes.

See Test scenarios for the list of planted mistakes.
